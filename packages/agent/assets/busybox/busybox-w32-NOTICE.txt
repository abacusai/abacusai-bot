busybox-w32
===========

This product ships an unmodified copy of the busybox-w32 executable and spawns
it as a separate process. It is not linked into, statically or dynamically, and
does not form a combined work with, any part of this product.

  Project:    busybox-w32 (a port of BusyBox to the Win32 API)
  Upstream:   https://frippery.org/busybox/
  Repository: https://github.com/rmyorston/busybox-w32
  Version:    FRP-6075-g169694ebd
  Licence:    GNU General Public License, version 2 (see busybox-w32-GPL-2.0.txt)
