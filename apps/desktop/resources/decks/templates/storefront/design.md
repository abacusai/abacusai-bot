# Storefront

Selling a product or service from a small business: cafes, shops, studios, agencies and local services pitching customers or partners. Has slides for the problem, three offerings, how it works, why us, three price tiers, a testimonial, proof in numbers, an offer and a contact close.

- Palette: warm cream with espresso ink, terracotta and a golden accent.
- Type: DM Serif Display for headings, DM Sans for text (friendly serif headlines over a round sans).
- Every slide carries a `data-label` naming its role, so a filler picks slides by what they are for.
- Avoid for: Formal finance, research reports and technical reviews.
