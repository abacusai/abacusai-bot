# Classroom

Teaching anything step by step: school lessons, staff training, workshops, onboarding modules and explainers. Has slides for objectives, a key definition, three building blocks, a worked example, a comparison, a big fact, an activity, a quick quiz and a recap.

- Palette: warm paper with deep teal headings and a sunny amber accent.
- Type: Fraunces for headings, Inter for text (soft serif headlines over a clean sans; readable from the back of a room).
- Every slide carries a `data-label` naming its role, so a filler picks slides by what they are for.
- Avoid for: Sales pitches, investor decks and moody brand storytelling: it reads as a classroom by design.
