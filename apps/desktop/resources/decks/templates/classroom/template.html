Key idea
Photosynthesis

The process plants use to turn light energy, water and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen inside their leaves.

The building blocks

Three things every leaf needs

01

Light

Sunlight gives the energy that drives the whole reaction.

02

Water

Roots draw it up from the soil to every leaf.

03

Carbon dioxide

Tiny pores in the leaf let it in from the air.

Worked example

Following one leaf through a sunny day

1

Morning light arrives

Chlorophyll in the leaf absorbs the light energy.

2

Water is split

The energy breaks water apart and oxygen is released.

3

Sugar is built

Carbon dioxide is joined into glucose for the plant.

Compare

Photosynthesis versus respiration

Photosynthesis

  • Happens only in light
  • Takes in carbon dioxide
  • Gives off oxygen
  • Stores energy as glucose

Respiration

  • Happens all the time
  • Takes in oxygen
  • Gives off carbon dioxide
  • Releases energy from glucose
Did you know
50%

Roughly half of the oxygen we breathe comes from tiny ocean plants called phytoplankton.
