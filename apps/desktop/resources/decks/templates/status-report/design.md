# Status Report

Reporting where work stands: project and program status, QBRs, steering committees, board and weekly updates. Has slides for an executive summary with an overall status, four KPIs, a workstream status table, a milestone timeline, progress bars, a risk table, decisions needed and next steps with owners and dates.

- Palette: clean white with slate ink, one indigo accent and green/amber/red status pills.
- Type: Inter Tight for headings, Inter for text (tight modern sans; tables and numbers read at a glance).
- Every slide carries a `data-label` naming its role, so a filler picks slides by what they are for.
- Avoid for: Inspirational keynotes, lifestyle brands and anything that should feel playful.
